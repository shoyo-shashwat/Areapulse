How co-verification works
When government submits proof that a civic issue is fixed, your NGO can independently verify the repair. Approve if the fix is satisfactory — the government officer can then officially close the issue. Reject if the fix is incomplete or the problem persists — the officer will be notified to re-inspect. Only government can mark an issue Resolved. Your role is independent verification.
